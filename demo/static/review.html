How to read a finding

Known attacks come from documented fraud typologies — FATF, Chainalysis, TRM. The evidence behind them is published.

Emerging threats were invented by the system and have no published evidence yet. Treat them as informed hypotheses, not established fact. Two badges matter here:

  • Written by the AI model, then checked — the model proposed this and the critic reproduced it against the real rulebook.
  • Coverage idea, not a fully tested gap — the scenario relies on something the rulebook has no field for, so parts of it could not be tested. Useful as a prompt for discussion, not as a proven evasion.
