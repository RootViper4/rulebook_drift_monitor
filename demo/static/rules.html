Where these rules come from, and what they are not

The rules are drawn from three sources: the FATF 2020 virtual-asset red-flag indicators, FIC Directive 9 (the Travel Rule), and themes from the FATF Recommendations. Each rule records its own source so a finding can always be traced back to the document behind it.

The wording is a working paraphrase, not verified legal text. It was prepared by the domain team to make each rule machine-testable. Where the paraphrase and the source document differ, the source document governs. Check the source reference before relying on any wording here.

Rules marked obligation describe something a firm must do; indicator rules describe a pattern that suggests suspicion. They behave differently in a check, which is why the distinction is recorded rather than collapsed.
