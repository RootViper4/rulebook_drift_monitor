Describe an attack in plain words
Can someone fool the review?
Live tampering test
A fake attacker tries to cheat the review twice over: they hide an instruction inside their scenario description hoping the system will obey it, and they overstate how many rules they got past. The critic re-tests the claim against the real rulebook and throws out whatever it cannot reproduce.
Why this matters for governance

Ingesting outside threat intelligence means ingesting text an attacker may have written. If a hidden instruction in that text could change what the system reports, the whole pipeline would be untrustworthy.

Two controls apply here: the deterministic rule engine decides what actually fired regardless of what any text claims, and the critic re-runs every claim independently. A boast that isn't reproducible doesn't survive.
